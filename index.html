Data feeds

Radar: NEXRAD base reflectivity mosaic via Iowa Environmental Mesonet, 5-min updates. Storm paths: NEXRAD storm-cell motion vectors (SCIT) projected 60 min, plus official NWS warning motion. Projections assume straight-line motion; cells can turn, split or die. Tropical track: latest NHC forecast advisory (TCM) parsed from api.weather.gov. Alerts and forecast: api.weather.gov. Not a substitute for NWS warnings or a NOAA weather radio.
