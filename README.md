# Storm Watch

Live weather radar and storm tracking for Western North Carolina, in one web page.

**Open it:** https://grittyhubby.github.io/storm-watch/

- NEXRAD radar loop (last 55 min)
- Storm-cell paths projected 60 min ahead, with arrival time and closest pass to your home
- NWS warnings for your exact location, plus official NWS storm tracks
- NHC tropical-system track for any active Atlantic storm
- Hourly forecast, French Broad River gauge, NC road closures (NCDOT DriveNC)

Tap **My GPS** or **Set home** once to center it on your house; it remembers on your phone.

Data: NOAA/NWS, NHC, Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NCDOT. Projections assume straight-line storm motion. Not a substitute for official NWS warnings or a NOAA weather radio.
